Key Indian Tax & Compliance Rules for YouTubers

1. Section 44ADA (50% Flat Expense Deduction)

Under Indian Income Tax Section 44ADA, freelancers and online creators with gross turnover up to ₹75 Lakhs only need to pay tax on 50% of gross receipts. The other 50% is automatically treated as deductible business expenses (camera gear, software, editing, studio, broadband) without submitting bills!

2. Zero GST with LUT (Export of Services)

YouTube payments from Google Ireland / Google LLC are classified as "Export of Services". By filing a free Letter of Undertaking (LUT) on the GST portal, Indian YouTubers pay 0% GST legally even if turnover exceeds ₹20 Lakhs.

3. US Tax W-8BEN & Foreign Tax Credit (FTC)

Because India has a Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA) with the US, submitting Form W-8BEN caps US withholding at 15% on US views only (instead of 30%). You can file Form 67 with your Indian ITR to claim this as a tax credit!

4. Advance Tax Deadlines

If your annual tax liability exceeds ₹10,000, you must pay Advance Tax in 4 quarterly installments (June 15, Sept 15, Dec 15, March 15) to avoid interest penalties under Sections 234B & 234C.
